2.0.0 (PS9-dev)
- PrestaShop 9 compatibility; corrected controller registration and removed old redirect API.
- Ignore a migrated advanced-stock flag instead of calling the removed dependsOnStock API.
- Authenticate payment callbacks before accessing orders; reject empty keys and malformed input.
- Share callback processing, serialize concurrent delivery, and prevent replay/double booking.
- Make payment, response, audit and status writes transactional; send status mail after commit.
- Validate currency/base units and module/shop ownership; reject ambiguous split orders.
- Preserve paid/terminal states on late callbacks and restrict retry links to pending Pays orders.
- Use random cart-bound checkout tokens and constant-time signature/token comparisons.
- Fix monetary epsilon, diagnostic-code storage, message interpolation and stale admin links.
- Remove the global checkout click blocker so validation retries and other methods work.
- Add unit, frontend and real PrestaShop 9 integration regression tests.
